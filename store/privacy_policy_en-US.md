# qScreenRec Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

SAISAI.Dev ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains how qScreenRec ("the app") handles your information.

## We collect no personal data
- The app requires **no account or sign-in** and **does not connect to the internet**.
- Your recordings are **saved only to the local folder you choose**. We cannot access them and they are never uploaded.
- The app contains no ads, tracking or analytics and uses no cookies.

## Settings stored on your PC
Your preferences (hotkey, format, watermark text, language, etc.) are stored only in the Windows registry on your PC so they can be restored next time. They are removed when you uninstall the Microsoft Store version.

## Screen capture
The app captures the screen only after you start a recording with the hotkey or menu, and stops as soon as you stop it or the time limit is reached. The tray icon blinks while recording.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page.
