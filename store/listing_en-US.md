# Store listing: English (United States)

## Product name
qScreenRec 螢幕畫面錄製

## Short description
A lightweight screen recorder that lives in your system tray. Press one hotkey to start, press it again to stop. Records your mouse cursor, adds an optional watermark, and lets you pick format and resolution.

## Description
qScreenRec is a system-tray screen recorder built for tutorials, bug reports and quick demos. There is no complicated window to open: press the hotkey (Ctrl+Alt+R by default) to start recording and press it again to save.

KEY FEATURES
• One-key recording: a global hotkey works even when the app is in the background
• Mouse included: records cursor movement and can highlight clicks
• Your format: MP4, MKV or animated GIF
• Your resolution: original, or 4K / 1440p / 1080p / 720p / 480p
• Time limit: stop automatically after a set number of minutes
• Text watermark: up to 20 characters, with position and opacity
• Hardware acceleration: uses NVIDIA, Intel or AMD GPU encoders automatically
• Multi-monitor: choose which screen to record
• English and Traditional Chinese interface, help on F1
• No sign-up, no internet required — recordings stay on your PC
