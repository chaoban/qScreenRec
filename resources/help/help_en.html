Getting started

  1. qScreenRec lives in the system tray at the bottom right of the taskbar (orange monitor icon). If you can't see it, click the "^" arrow to show hidden icons.
  2. Press {{HOTKEY}} to start recording (a 3-second countdown is shown by default).
  3. While recording, the tray icon blinks red. Hover over it to see the elapsed time.
  4. Press {{HOTKEY}} again to stop. The video is saved automatically and a notification appears; click it to show the file in File Explorer.

FAQ

Q: Nothing happens when I press the hotkey.
Another program (for example a screenshot or gaming tool) may be using it. Choose another hotkey in Settings → General.

Q: Will the hotkey also trigger something in other programs?
No. Once registered, the hotkey belongs to qScreenRec and the foreground program (for example a browser) does not receive it. If another program already owns the combination, you are asked to pick another one at startup. The default is Ctrl+Alt+R, which avoids the browser hard-refresh shortcut Ctrl+Shift+R.

Q: The video is black.
Some DRM-protected players and content are hidden from capture by Windows.

Q: The recording stutters or files are too big.
Lower the resolution to 1080p or 720p, use 30 fps, or set quality to Medium.

Q: Administrator prompts (UAC) are not recorded.
Windows does not allow capturing the secure desktop; the video holds the previous frame meanwhile.

Q: My save folder is on drive D:, what happens on a PC without it?
The recording is saved to "Videos\qScreenRec" instead, and the notification shows where.

Q: What if the disk fills up?
At least 300 MB must be free to start. If free space drops below 150 MB while recording, the recording stops and is saved safely.
